# G4.3 — Egress discipline: the senior verdict (no-retry, the 2-rung cap, family visitors, the edge negative cache, and the official-player lane)

**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Branch:** `ws-w/g4-residential-direct` (iterating on #167) · **Extends:** G4-residential-direct.md, G4.2-sabr-protection-wall.md

## The mandate

After G4.2 the phone reported the burnout curve in the wild: search
"Faded" (licensed) → error; "Timeless" (licensed) → **played in full**;
then 4–5 more licensed tracks → every one refused:

```
no rung resolved 5EpyN_6dqyk
· [VISIONOS*]  LOGIN_REQUIRED @relay-egress
· [WEB+POT*]   attempt 3: LOGIN_REQUIRED
· [IOS+POT*]   LOGIN_REQUIRED @relay-egress
· [IOS*]       player call relay 403
· [VISIONOS]   LOGIN_REQUIRED
· [WEB_SAFARI] LOGIN_REQUIRED
· visitor=relay
```

The problem was framed to the senior engineer as an architecture consult
(five measured walls: the CORS wall, the IP-class/token paradox, egress
convergence, the SABR cutoffs, behavioral flagging). His verdict, in full,
drives this phase:

1. **A foreign-origin SPA cannot be a direct zero-credential innertube
   client for licensed music.** `youtubei.googleapis.com` and googlevideo's
   `initplayback` are CORS-allowlisted to youtube.com's own origins only —
   every control-plane call funnels through the shared Cloudflare Worker
   (AS13335), collapsing all users × all 6 rungs × 3 flap-retries onto one
   datacenter egress: an **18× request amplifier per failed track** that
   trips YouTube's abuse classifiers within 1–2 tracks.
2. **LOGIN_REQUIRED is a deterministic policy verdict, not a flap.**
   Retrying it — and morphing one visitorData across VISIONOS → WEB → IOS
   → WEB_SAFARI within seconds — reinforces the bot signal.
3. **Licensed SABR continuation is upstream protocol work we must not
   own** over a datacenter relay (the G4.2 circuit breakers stay; no
   Shaka-class attestation engine).
4. **The viable lanes:** the native app shell (its own IP, no CORS) for
   the custom engine; the official IFrame Player for the pure web; BYOC
   rejected (account-ban + XSS liability), OAuth Device/PKCE the only
   sanctioned auth lane if one is ever wanted.

## What changed (this PR)

### 1. The egress-discipline ladder (`audio/resolve.ts`)

- **LOGIN_REQUIRED never retries.** Every rung (and the WEB+POT loop)
  breaks on the first LR verdict; the G4/G4.1 flap-retry policy is dead.
- **The Worker-egress rung cap = 2.** At most two relay-egress player
  calls per resolve; the remaining rungs SKIP with an honest verdict
  (`worker-egress rung cap (2) reached`). Direct-lane rungs (native
  bridge / healed posture) are uncapped and keep the G4.1 two-attempt
  policy.
- **A relay-egress attempt is single-shot** (no flap-retry on the shared
  IP) — inside `runRawRung` (raw rungs) and via `egressLaneFor` (the
  WEB+POT funnel call).
- **The bot-gate handoff marker.** When the gate killed the ladder the
  terminal error carries `bot-gate=1`; `isBotGateError()` is the UI's
  trigger for the IFrame lane.
- **In the plain browser the effective ladder is exactly rung 1
  (VISIONOS*, tokenless, app visitor) → rung 2 (WEB+POT, the licensed
  primary) → honest gate diagnosis.** Two player POSTs per gated track
  (plus the once-per-12 h mint legs on a cold page), versus 18+.

### 2. Per-client-family visitors (`lib/ytSession.ts`)

- Two families, two identities: **web** (the youtubei.js session, the
  mint root, WEB_SAFARI) and **app** (the raw VISIONOS/IOS rungs). Each
  born via `/youtubei/v1/config` on the calling egress, persisted
  (web 30 d direct / 7 d relay; app 14 d), never shared across families.
- **Rotation on a persistent LOGIN_REQUIRED** — once per page load per
  family (`rotateVisitorFamily`): the gated family's visitor is dropped
  and re-born on the next resolve; the web rotation also drops the
  session singleton and the visitor-bound POT cache.
- The terminal diagnosis now reports both: `visitor=relay/app=relay`.

### 3. The edge negative cache (`relay/src/index.ts`)

The Worker-side half of the discipline: a player-POST whose upstream
answer is `playabilityStatus.status === "LOGIN_REQUIRED"` is cached at
the edge (Cache API, per-colo, **30 min**), keyed by
`(client, poToken-present, videoId)` — a retried gated track fails
closed in <1 ms and the shared IP never sees the repeat. Deliberately
negative-only (positive verdicts carry per-visitor session state);
streaming is untouched for everything that is not a player POST — media
bytes never buffer. Bypass: `X-Relay-NegCache: off` (dropped upstream);
the `/` capability echo gains `neg-cache`. **The relay requires a manual
redeploy** (`wrangler deploy`) — CI proves the bundle, deployment is by
design manual (see wrangler.toml).

### 4. The official-player fallback lane (`audio/iframeLane.ts` + `components/IframeLanePanel.tsx`)

When `isBotGateError(message)` fires, HomeScreen hands the track to the
official YouTube IFrame Player: a genuine youtube.com origin iframe on
the user's residential egress, YouTube's own attestation and SABR
handshake, licensed music playing **in full**. The engine parks
honestly; the row badge says where the track went; embedding-disabled
videos (error 101/150) surface an "Open on YouTube" link. Honest scope:
a per-track delegate, not a queue participant; YouTube's own ads/branding
ride along — that is the price and the point of the official player.

### 5. The native-shell HTTP bridge (`lib/ytSession.ts`)

The Q1 verdict's custom-engine lane: `window.streamifyNative.fetch(url,
{method, headers, body}) → {status, headers, body}` — HTTP on the OS
network stack, the phone's own IP, no CORS preflight. Every direct-class
googleapis call in the funnel (`ytFetch`, `directPlayerPost`, the
visitor births) checks for the bridge first; without it (the plain web
build) the G4.1 CORS-lane + relay-fallback behavior is unchanged. The
shell side (the APK / iOS host implementing the bridge) is the follow-up
this contract unblocks — under it, the direct rungs finally ride the
phone's IP, uncapped, with residential-class trust.

## What did NOT change

- The G4.2 SABR circuit breakers (8 s first-media budget, 6× no-media
  abort) — the senior's explicit keep.
- The mint (once per ~12 h, relay egress, single envelope).
- The relay's allowlist, envelope pinning, UA matrix, streaming
  contract (206 fidelity, no media buffering).
- No warmup farms, no synthetic engagement, no BYOC — all rejected by
  the verdict.

## Honest limits

- The pure-web licensed path is now **the official embed** — the custom
  engine still cannot stream licensed music from the shared Worker
  egress, and no client-side discipline changes that IP-class fact; the
  cap + cache + no-retry keep the egress *healthy* (standard content
  keeps resolving; the relay stops bleeding), they do not unlock
  licensed bytes.
- The 30 min negative-verdict TTL is the senior's window, not a measured
  YouTube decay constant; the nightly canary can measure the real
  recovery horizon now that retries are off (each canary request is a
  clean sample).
- The IFrame lane is per-track: the queue/bar do not drive it (a queue
  participant needs the embed's `onStateChange` → engine-queue wiring —
  a small follow-up if the lane proves out on the phone).
- The native bridge is contract + client side only in this PR; the
  shells must implement it before the direct rungs leave the relay.
